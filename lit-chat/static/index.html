What's protected — and what isn't

  • Protected: your chat history is ciphertext everywhere outside the enclave. Keys are derived inside the TEE from your session and never stored. The at-rest envelope is symmetric-only (AES-256-GCM), which is why the stored-history claim is post-quantum.
  • Protected: sessions are signed inside the enclave. A database operator cannot mint one.
  • Not protected: while a response is generated, your prompt is sent to the selected external model provider (zero-data-retention routes only). "Prompts never leave the enclave" arrives with in-enclave models.
  • Not protected: traffic metadata (timing, sizes) and — if you pay later — payment records.
  • Anonymous sessions: the cookie in this browser IS the capability. It cannot be revoked or recovered. Deleting it orphans this history permanently.
  • Deletion: deleting a conversation removes it immediately; encrypted copies persist in database backups until backup expiry (target ≤ 30 days). We do not use the phrase "crypto-shred".
